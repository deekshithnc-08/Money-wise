YOUR INFORMATION, HANDLED WITH CARE

Privacy & referral notice

This site collects information for Money Wise Women programme enquiries, booking requests and referral attribution. By submitting a form, you agree to the use described here.

What we collect

Booking requests include your name, mobile number, email, city, consent and the time of submission. Referral links include your name and, if provided, your mobile number. When mobile verification is enabled, Twilio processes your number and verification messages.

How we use it

The myPAL team uses your contact details to follow up about the programme, manage booking and offline payment records, and understand which channels and referrers brought participants. We do not collect card details or process online payments on this site. Submitting a request does not confirm a seat.

Referral attribution and cookies

A first-party browser cookie lasts up to 30 days and remembers your first valid referral and latest campaign source. Visits are counted anonymously by browser; they do not tell us who forwarded a message. A shared link credits its original owner. The booking form allows you to remove referral credit before submitting. Your referrer's name appears to people visiting their link. Referrers cannot access participant details or the admin dashboard.

Access and security

Only authenticated administrators can view booking, contact and payment records. Admin sessions use a separate cookie that expires after eight hours. Records are stored in the programme database. Any downloaded reports must be handled with the same care as the dashboard.

Your choices

You may browse without submitting contact details and clear cookies in your browser. You may ask the myPAL team handling your programme enquiry to correct or delete your record. Before public launch, myPAL must publish its designated privacy contact and retention period here.

Launch configuration required: the programme owner must add a privacy contact and a retention schedule before collecting public registrations.
