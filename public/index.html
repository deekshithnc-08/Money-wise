A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Curious? Good.

Who is this programme for?

Educated, aspiring women and women entrepreneurs who want to build practical financial confidence. No prior finance expertise is needed.

When and where are the sessions?

The team will share confirmed dates, venue and fees before you commit. Submit a booking request to hear about the upcoming cohort.

Do I need to book before referring someone?

No. Anyone can create a personal referral link. Your friends' booking requests will appear under your name in the private myPAL dashboard.

How are forwarded links tracked?

The original owner of the link gets credit, even if it is forwarded. A friend must generate and share their own link to receive credit. We retain the first valid referral for 30 days in the same browser; anonymous visits do not identify individual people.
